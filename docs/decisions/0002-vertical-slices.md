# ADR 0002 — Vertical capabilities and explicit qualification profiles

Status: accepted design direction; implementation remains pending.

## Context

A component-oriented sequence can produce a broker, ledger, scheduler and desktop without demonstrating useful work. A single final gate covering every future capability can delay feedback indefinitely. Long autonomous sessions need a finite scope and durable continuity.

## Decision

P01 executes a real bounded local operation through admission and effect recording. Subsequent packages extend the integrated path. M1 delivers a local workspace with human interaction and recovery. AI, distribution, federation, accelerators and named ecosystem integrations have additional gates.

Qualify explicit profiles with version, environment and scope. P14 may qualify the local-runtime profile before the distribution or federated profiles exist. Independent reproduction targets a submitted revision. Specification validation is a separate claim.

Agents work continuously across commits within a recorded mandate and preserve checkpoints at session boundaries. A single coordinator can serialize integration; a new orchestration system is unnecessary to begin.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

Early interfaces expose fewer but real capabilities. Packages may share a PR; modules may share a process. Qualification occurs at meaningful boundaries. Missing hardware blocks only its profile. Revisit package boundaries when evidence shows duplication, dependency cycles or a gate that cannot stand alone. Preserve or explicitly migrate acceptance IDs and failure history.
