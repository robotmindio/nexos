# E04 — Delegate bounded work and revoke it during execution

Qualification: not implemented. This is an acceptance specification, not a record of an executed test.

## Given

Two isolated workers have separate authenticated identities and receive different scoped grants from one parent. A finite enforceable local resource budget and explicit external cost-estimate policy are configured.

## When

**E04-T1.** Delegate distinct operations/targets and inspect the grant tree, budgets and expiry.

**E04-T2.** Run real authorized work concurrently and inspect its receipts and reservation ledger.

**E04-T3.** Revoke one child during a stream of operations and wait for enforcement acknowledgement.

**E04-T4.** Inspect allowed remaining work, denied new effects and already-dispatched outcomes separately.

## Then

Authority is enforced independently of model cooperation. A child cannot inherit broad user credentials, enlarge the grant or consume the sibling’s reservation. Revocation does not erase committed work.

## Required negative and interruption paths

**E04-N1.** Attempt grant amplification, identity forgery, direct host/network bypass and secret access from the worker.

**E04-N2.** Race two reservations at the remaining limit and race dispatch with revocation.

**E04-N3.** Remove a required isolation facility: the claimed profile fails admission rather than running with a weaker boundary.

## Evidence

Grant chain and authenticated identities; actual adversarial worker commands; reservation race results; dispatch/revocation order; target state and denied-effect records.

Use the environment/claim rules in [ACCEPTANCE.md](../ACCEPTANCE.md). The implementing package supplies executable commands and assertions as the real implementation is written; no illustrative command here is represented as an existing CLI.

## Variants and guarantee limits

Baseline workers can be deterministic adversarial processes. An AI deployment repeats relevant tests with real model-produced requests; model refusal alone never proves containment.
