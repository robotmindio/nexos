# E02 — Resume durable work after client and worker failure

Qualification: not implemented. This is an acceptance specification, not a record of an executed test.

## Given

A real multi-step task writes an artifact, records a checkpoint and performs an effect through a provider with declared idempotency/reconciliation semantics. Task identity, authority and budget are durable. A second authenticated client can inspect the same local authority.

## When

**E02-T1.** Start the task, disconnect the first client and confirm that work continues independently where intended.

**E02-T2.** Kill the worker and restart the runtime at selected checkpoint/dispatch boundaries.

**E02-T3.** Open a second client and resume the task from its compatible checkpoint after revalidating authority.

**E02-T4.** Inspect completed work, outstanding liability and receipts; complete the task without repeating a committed idempotent effect.

## Then

Task identity survives process/client loss. Attempt identities change when appropriate. Checkpoints and effects, not opaque process-memory migration, explain progress. Unknown external outcome remains visible until reconciliation.

## Required negative and interruption paths

**E02-N1.** Lose the provider response after a non-idempotent dispatch: resume quarantines the effect instead of blindly retrying.

**E02-N2.** Revoke or expire the grant before resume: inspection may remain allowed but new effects are denied.

**E02-N3.** Crash during reservation settlement: unresolved liability remains reserved and a concurrent task cannot spend it.

## Evidence

Task/attempt/checkpoint identities; real worker kill/restart commands; provider-visible invocation count and results; authority checks; accounting before/after failure.

Use the environment/claim rules in [ACCEPTANCE.md](../ACCEPTANCE.md). The implementing package supplies executable commands and assertions as the real implementation is written; no illustrative command here is represented as an existing CLI.

## Variants and guarantee limits

Baseline uses two clients of one authority. Transfer to another executor/node is E07 and must not be inferred from the local restart test.
